I read <a target="_self" href="http://www.sitepoint.com/blogs/2007/11/08/firefox-bug-status-bar-zaniness/ ">Firefox Bug: Status Bar Zaniness</a> and he briefly mentions <a target="_self" href="http://www.sitepoint.com/dustmeselectors/">Dust Me Selector</a>, a Firefox extention, but didn't mention what it does.
