<h2>Stump the CFChump 2b</h2>
<h3>Question </h3>
What will display when this code is ran?


```coldfusion
<cfset myvar = "Hello ">

<cfif 1 eq true>
	<cfset myvar = myvar & "Carrie">
<cfelseif 1 eq false>
	<cfset myvar = myvar & "Brody">
<cfelse>
	<cfset myvar = myvar & "Saul">
</cfif>

<cfoutput>#myvar#</cfoutput> 
```


<h3>Answers</h3>

A. Hello Carrie<br />
B. Hello Brody<br />
C. Hello Saul<br />
D. Error
