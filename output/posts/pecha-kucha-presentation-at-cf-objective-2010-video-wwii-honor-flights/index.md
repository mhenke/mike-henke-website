Here is an "extra" part of the <a href="http://www.cfobjective.com">CF.Objective()</a> 2010 conference I attended in Minneapolis. I was asked to present a <a href="http://www.pecha-kucha.org/what">Pecha Kucha</a> along with <a href="http://www.silverwareconsulting.com/index.cfm/2010/4/6/cfObjective-Pecha-Kucha-BOF--On-Like-Donkey-Kong">8 other guys</a>. A Pecha Kucha is a presentation with 20 slides, rotating automatically after 20 seconds. I decided to do it over the <a href="http://www.semnhonorflight.org/">MN Southeast Honor Flights.</a> I was busy working on my <a href="http://www.cfwheels.org">ColdFusion On Wheels</a> session that occurred earlier in the day so my Pecha Kucha presentation was off the cuff and not practiced. I had a great time and thanks to <a href="http://www.silverwareconsulting.com/">Bob Silverberg</a> for putting the PK event together. Enjoy!
