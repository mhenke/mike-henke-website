My company recently hired another developer. It was the first time I was involved in the hiring process. We hired a recent college graduate with no CFML experience and slight PHP. The grad did seem somewhat strong with HTML, SQL, and CSS. Javascript was slight and jQuery non-existant. I'll be doing blog series on the training as it progressing.
We will focus on <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ColdFusion">CFML</a> the first week, with a little <a href="http://jquery.com/">jQuery</a> at the end of the week. Then the second week we will focus on <a href="http://git-scm.com/">Git</a>, SQL, and a little CSS. Training will ideally be broken into half days with learning the company, application, and ticket system. The other half day will be learning <a href="http://www.adobe.com/products/coldfusion-family.html">Adobe ColdFusion</a>, <a href="http://www.adobe.com/products/coldfusion-builder.html">ColdFusion Builder</a> and other programming skills.
Here is the <a href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1qvIlIze26xZnY3nJnmGG8Yg31huxD_8-zxB434gAKKY/edit">training material and schedule</a>. I also gave the developer a copy of <a href="http://www.amazon.com/ColdFusion-Developer-Tutorial-John-Farrar/dp/1847194125/ref=sr_1_2?ie=UTF8&amp;qid=1337091366&amp;sr=8-2">ColdFusion 8 Developer Tutorial</a>. We are using ACF9 but didn't have a copy of the updated book. I also picked up <a href="http://www.amazon.com/Railo-Beginners-Guide-Mark-Drew/dp/1849513406/ref=sr_1_1?ie=UTF8&amp;qid=1337091542&amp;sr=8-1">Railo 3 Beginner's Guide</a>.
This opportunity is a great chance to try <a href="/blog/is-adobe-coldfusion-web-application-construction-kit-series-bad-for-new-cfml-developers/">my recommended CFML books for newbies</a>.
