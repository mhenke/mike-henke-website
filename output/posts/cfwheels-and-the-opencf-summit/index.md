I was asked to lead an Unconference session on <a href="http://www.cfwheels.org">CFWheels</a> at the <a href="http://www.opencfsummit.org/">OpenCF Summit</a>. They are hoping to have a session for each of the major MVC frameworks. It should be a great time to learn and hang out with fellow ColdFusion developers. The conference is in Dallas, TX from February 21-23. If you can still make it, the <a href="http://www.brownpapertickets.com/event/137534">registration</a> is less than 40 bucks for a 3 day conference with 1 track <a href="http://www.opencfsummit.org/index.cfm/schedule/">schedule</a>. Hope to see you there!
