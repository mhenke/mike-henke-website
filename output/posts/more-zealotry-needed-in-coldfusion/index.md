I hear the word <strong>zealotry </strong>used and for me it has a <strong>negative </strong>connotation. It has been brought up lately usually around something perceived as <em>new </em>but that doesn't mean something <em>old </em>could have zealotry. <em></em>
<h2><em>I would like to see more zealotry in ColdFusion.</em></h2>
I thought it would be nice to break-down the definition of "zealotry" using <a href="http://dictionary.reference.com">dictionary.reference.com</a>.
<ul>
<li><a href="http://dictionary.reference.com/browse/zealotry">zeal·ot·ry</a> - <strong><em>undue </em></strong>or <strong>excessive</strong> <strong>zeal</strong>; fanaticism.</li>
<li><a href="http://dictionary.reference.com/browse/undue">un·due</a>
<ul>
<li>unwarranted; <strong><em>excessive</em></strong>: undue haste.</li>
<li>inappropriate; <strong><em>unjustifiable</em></strong>; improper: undue influence.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><a href="http://dictionary.reference.com/browse/excessive">ex·ces·sive</a> - <strong><em>going beyond the usual</em></strong>, necessary, or proper limit or degree; characterized by excess.</li>
<li><a href="http://dictionary.reference.com/browse/zeal">zeal</a> - fervor for a person, cause, or object; <strong><em>eager desire</em></strong> or endeavor; <strong><em>enthusiastic </em><em>diligence</em></strong>; ardor.</li>
</ul>
With this break-down, my preceived negatives are "<em>excessive</em>" and "<em>undo</em>". <em><strong>Undue </strong></em>could be <strong>negative </strong>for me since it is "<em>unjustifiable</em>" but I wonder how many of the "zealotry" claims lately are truly on an <strong>unjustifiable</strong> statement.
<strong><em>Excessive </em></strong>may not be <strong>negative </strong>depending on what a person considers "<em>going beyond the usual</em>". 
<ul>
<li>Is reading a programming book "excessive"?</li>
<li>Is blogging "excessive"?</li>
<li>Is presenting on a subject "excessive"?</li>
<li>Is working on an open source project "excessive"? </li>
</ul>
Sadly, i would say "Yes" these are <strong>excessive</strong>. These are "<em>going beyond the usual</em>". 
Are these <strong>negative</strong>? No. Next time, when I hear <strong>zealotry </strong>used, I'll try to keep this in mind. <em>Was the zealotry claim on some "<strong>unjustifiable</strong>" statement?</em> In most cases, I doubt the actual instance called <em>zealtory </em>was <em>"</em>unjustifiable<em>" </em>so it isn't negative but a <strong>positive </strong>case of zealotry.
 In this post's context, I think ColdFusion needs more <strong>zealots</strong> which means "<strong>going beyond the usual</strong>" with "<strong>enthusiastic diligence</strong>" using CFML.
