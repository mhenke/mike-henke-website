<big>next <a href="http://www.necfug.com/">NECFUG</a> - Selenium with Coldfusion and MXUnit</big>
<h3 style="color: red">Next meeting: May 27th, 6pm</h3>
Tagline: Sometimes it pays to stay in bed in Monday, rather than spending the rest of the week debugging Monday's code. - Dan Salomon
If you ever felt like the tag line, this meeting might help. I will be covering <a href="http://selenium.openqa.org/">Selenium</a> testing with a ColdFusion flare. I'll introduce Selenium, cover the <a href="http://selenium-ide.openqa.org/">Firefox IDE</a>, incorporate a test into <a href="http://mxunit.org/">Mxunit</a>, and hopefully demo a <a href="http://selenium-grid.openqa.org/">Selenium Grid</a>. Bring your laptops since this will be hands on.
Free Pizza and usually door prizes. <a href="http://www.cfwebtools.com">CF WebTools</a> hosts the meetings and is located at 11204 Davenport, near I680 &amp; Dodge. Discussion and pizza start around 6:00, with the presentation starting shortly after.
