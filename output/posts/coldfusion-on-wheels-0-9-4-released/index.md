<a href="http://cfwheels.org/download">CFWheels released 0.9.4</a> today if you haven't heard. I really enjoy using CFWheels because of the conventions and it was designed for <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coldfusion">ColdFusion </a>based on Rails ideas. <a href="http://cfwheels.org/">ColdFusion on Wheels</a> provides an easy way for rapid development plus it has a built, simple to use Object-relational mapping (<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Object-relational_mapping">ORM</a>).<a href="http://groups.google.com/group/cfwheels/browse_thread/thread/9eed1f55279ca08b">Here is the notice</a> on the <a href="http://groups.google.com/group/cfwheels">CFWheels Google Group</a>. They also <a href="http://www.cfwheels.org/blog/index.cfm/2009/9/15/Released-Today-ColdFusion-on-Wheels-094">posted an announcement on the CFWheels blog</a>.
