<h2>Stump the CFChump 1</h2>
<h3>Question </h3>

What will display when this code is ran?


```coldfusion
<cfset myvar = 4>
<cfoutput>
#++myvar# - #myvar--#
</cfoutput>
```



<h3>Answers</h3>
A. 5 - 4
B. 5 - 5
C. 4 - 3
