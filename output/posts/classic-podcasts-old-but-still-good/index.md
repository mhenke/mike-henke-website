I stumbled across <a href="http://www.hanselminutes.com">Hanselminutes</a> awhile ago. It is usually a well organized and informative podcast.
<span style="font-size: 10pt">What is Hanselminutes?</span></br>
Hanselminutes is a weekly audio talk show with noted web developer and technologist Scott Hanselman and hosted by Carl Franklin. Scott discusses utilities and tools, gives practical how-to advice, and discusses ASP.NET or Windows issues and workarounds.
Here are a couple I burned and enjoy listening to.
