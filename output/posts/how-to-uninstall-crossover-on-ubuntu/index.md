I installed <a href="http://www.codeweavers.com/products/crossover/">CrossOver</a> to test it on Ubuntu but the uninstall link from the menu didn't work.
I had to go to <strong><em>/opt/cxoffice/bin/</em></strong> then run: <strong><em>sudo ./cxuninstall</em></strong>
<em>mhenke@mhenke-Studio-1737:/opt/cxoffice/bin$ sudo ./cxuninsta</em>ll
And it is gone!
