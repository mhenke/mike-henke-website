This is a pre-<a href="http://www.cfobjective.com/">CF.Objective</a> 2 day workshop on May 10-11 in Minneapolis, MN.
<a href="http://cfwheels.org/">ColdFusion On Wheels</a> is a powerful web application framework that has become popular in the past few years. It is relatively easy to develop with and can be used for awesome sites like <a href="http://amtrakguestrewards.com/">AmtrakGuestRewards.com</a>, or other cool ones like <a href="http://meritbuilder.com/">Meritbuilder.com</a> and <a href="http://singsnap.com/">Singsnap.com</a>.
You’ll spend the two days working through a single concept by creating a simple blog system and learning the basics of ColdFusion On Wheels including:
<ul>
<li>Models, Views, and Controllers</li>
<li>Data Structures &amp; Relationships</li>
<li>Routing</li>
<li>Migrations</li>
<li>Views with forms, partials, and helpers</li>
<li>RESTful design</li>
<li>Using Wheels plugins</li>
</ul>
