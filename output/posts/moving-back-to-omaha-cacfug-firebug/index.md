Well, if you follow my blog you may remember I <a href="/blog/dc-or-bust/">moved to Washington, DC</a> nearly 2 years ago. It has been an interesting time. I have seen <a href="http://www.thingstododc.com/">many embassies</a>, been on lots of <a href="http://www.washingtonwalks.com">walking tours</a>, and many boat cruises on the Potomac along with seeing all the memorials and some Smithsonians. I really enjoyed the <a href="http://www.semnhonorflight.org/">Southeast Minnesota Honor flights</a>. I think I helped with five. With that said, I am moving back to Omaha after accepting a position that looks very interesting and will allow me to mentor a younger developer while hopefully implementing a lot of <a href="http://www.cfwheels.org">Wheels projects</a> on new applications while expanding the position and leading more developers as the company grows. 
<h2>CACFUG</h2>
I would like to point out a new user group, I will be missing. The <a href="http://www.dc-coldfusion.com/">Capital Area ColdFusion User Group</a> has had an outstanding start and I am sad I will be unable to attend all the upcoming meetings. They have a lot of great content and presenters to pick from in the DMV (District, Maryland, and Virgina -- Washington loves its initializations). The CACFUG reminded me of the <a href="http://www.necfug.com/">NECFUG</a> I left and will be rejoining in Omaha. The members are really friendly and smart. The upcoming meeting on Nov 9th will be my last for the CACFUG.
<h2>Firebug/CSS</h2>
The job I am leaving asked me to give a quick Firebug/CSS demo before I leave, so here are some resources. I would watch this for an <a href="http://getfirebug.com/video/Intro2FB.mp4">intro into firebug</a>. I use the net and html tabs the most for css/html editing/debugging. I have used the Script tab a little to debug javascript. As for learning CSS, I would say I learned a lot from this book, <a href="http://www.amazon.com/Head-First-HTML-CSS-XHTML/dp/059610197X">Head First XHTML/CSS</a>. And for<a href="http://www.amazon.com/jQuery-Action-Second-Bear-Bibeault/dp/1935182323"> jQuery I like the “In Action” book series</a>
