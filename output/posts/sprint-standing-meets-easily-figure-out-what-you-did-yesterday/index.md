We started Agile development at my work. We are on the third sprint. Today before our standing meeting, I mentioned to a co-worker, "<em>I needed to figure out what I did yesterday</em>." If you aren't familiar with a sprint standing meeting, you need to cover three items: 1) what you did yesterday 2) what you are going to do today 3) any roadblocks
I forgot I can easily use <a href="http://www.tasktop.com">Tasktop</a>'s "Time Tracking Dashboard" to pull up what I did in the IDE yesterday.
<img src="http://mikehenke.com/assets/content/yesterday.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="400" />
 
Then I realized, I had to do a simple custom report for yesterday. Wouldn't it be even easier if Tasktop had a "Yesterday" radio button along with the current "Today", "This Week" and "Last 4 weeks"?
<img src="http://mikehenke.com/assets/content/yesterday2.jpg" alt="" width="640" height="400" />
With <a href="http://www.eclipse.org/mylyn/">Mylyn</a> submitting a ticket to Tasktop is a breeze. I created a new task in the Tasktop repository, filled out the ticket in my IDE and submitted it to Tasktop. <strong>Never leaving Eclipse. </strong>I hope <a href="http://www.adobe.com/products/coldfusion-builder/features.html">Adobe ColdFusion Builder</a> and <a href="http://www.cfeclipse.org">CFEclipse</a> start providing Mylyn and their task repository automatically.
