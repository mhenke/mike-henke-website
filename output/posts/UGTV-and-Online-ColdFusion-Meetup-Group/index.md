Charlie Arehart has been very busy lining up great <a target="_self" href="http://coldfusion.meetup.com/17/">Online ColdFusion Meetup Group</a> meetings. If you don't know about the group it is an awesome learning resource. Charile has setup a site, <a target="_self" href="http://www.carehart.org/ugtv/">UGTV</a>, for finding ANY usergroup recorded presentions. UGTV holds links to the recordings along with a title, description, time, presenter, and date. UGTV is great if you miss a presentation or want to share one. Anyone can submit urls.
