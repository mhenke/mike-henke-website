With another outstanding <a href="http://cfobjective.com/">CFObjective</a> over, I would like to mention <a href="http://d2wc.com/">d2w</a> is coming up in July. This conference will have familiar faces from CFObjective and CFUnited along with many others since this conference focuses on <strong>Workflow</strong> for <em>Developers</em> and <em>Designers</em>. Sessions are geared toward improving workflow. And what would a Workflow conference be without <a href="http://www.eclipse.org/mylyn/">Mylyn</a> / <a href="http://www.tasktop.com">TaskTop</a> being covered, so kicking off the first sessions, I will be presenting "<a href="http://d2wc.com/speakers/mike-henke">A Task-Oriented Workflow with Mylyn and Eclipse</a>".
Also in July, another 2 Day <a href="http://cfwheels-training.stagehq.com/events/769">Introduction into ColdFusion On Wheels</a> will be held.
