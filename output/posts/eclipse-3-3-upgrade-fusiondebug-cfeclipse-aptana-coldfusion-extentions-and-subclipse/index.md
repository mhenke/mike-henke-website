I finally am upgraded to Eclipse 3.3. Everything went smooth with FusionDebug, CFEclipse, Aptana, and Subclipse. I did stumble a little with finding the current <a href="http://download.macromedia.com/pub/coldfusion/8/eclipseextensions/cf8_extensions_for_eclipse.zip" target="_self">ColdFusion Extentions</a> so here is the direct link. My old archived site pointed to CF_FB_Extensions.zip and the new one is called cf8_extensions_for_eclipse.zip. Hope this helps someone :-) FYI - I did get the direct link from comment posted on Eclipse 3.3 and the <a href="http://www.cfreport.org/index.cfm/2007/8/17/Eclipse-33-and-the-ColdFusion-Extensions-for-Eclipse" target="_self">ColdFusion Extensions for Eclipse</a> entry for CFReport.org's blog.
